Bloom and Bloom Mailer are WordPress tools I built for collecting subscribers and sending campaigns without forcing the whole workflow into a separate platform.

Bloom handles opt-in forms and subscriber collection. Bloom Mailer handles campaigns, sending queues, events, and automations, with integrations for popular email platforms when needed.

The goal is simple: keep the workflow close to WordPress while still giving site owners room to connect the tools they already use.
